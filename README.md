# kasiAIhub website

The website behind kasiaihub.com: a practical AI business-systems training programme for South African community entrepreneurs. Entrepreneurs build a working system for their own business, with guidance, instead of watching slides.

## What is in it

- Marketing site: home, about, the Entrepreneur AI Journey (three build sessions), pricing, FAQ, resources.
- Blog: each article is its own route with its own metadata, structured data and FAQ.
- Business Check: a free diagnostic of about 12 questions that shows which business system an owner needs most, with a downloadable report.
- Enrolment flow with preferred weekday and class-size handling.
- Admin views for diagnostics, the waitlist and the community study.
- Waitlist for Kasi Move, an ordering platform for food vendors.
- Transactional email templates (React Email): sign-in, recovery, diagnostic report, waitlist signup.
- Rate limiting and a fail-closed claim step around the AI-written parts of the diagnostic report.

## Stack

React, TanStack Start / Router / Query, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI, react-hook-form with Zod, Supabase (auth and data), Drizzle ORM migrations, Vitest.
